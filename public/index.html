Time Travel Heist — a puzzle game for Android

Step through a locked door. Then go back in time and unlock it before you get there.

Time Travel Heist is a turn-based puzzle game for Android where you are your own accomplice. Every vault gives you a handful of turns, but never enough for one pair of hands, so you step into a time portal, rewind to the moment you arrived, and live those same turns again as a second, third or even fourth self.

When you travel back in time, your earlier selves are still there, living out their own timelines — so they can throw the switch on a door you need to walk through, disarm a detector a moment before you walk into it, or carry a screwdriver back through the portal so that you are holding it before you reach down to pick it up. Three portals give you four selves and four times the turns, all of them moving at once.

Once you have woven all your timelines together, they must unlock every door you walk through, disarm every detector and unscrew every duct cover you crawl through.

The story

In 1928, you pick up a radio signal from five centuries away: a resistance movement losing a war against the banks. The same banks you can see from your window, grown by then into terrifying megabanks. The resistance must start the fight in your time, and for that they need you.

Who should you trust? And why will nobody tell you what happens in 1929?

What you get

More than 50 hand-built levels across nine chapters, from a single-room tutorial to vaults that will take you an evening. It plays offline, there are no ads and nothing to buy.
