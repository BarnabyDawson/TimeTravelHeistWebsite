Time Travel Heist

A puzzle game for Android

Step through a locked door. Then go back in time and unlock it before you get there.

Time Travel Heist is a turn-based puzzle game for Android where your only accomplice is yourself. Every vault gives you a handful of turns and it is never enough, so you step into a time portal, rewind to the moment you arrived, and live those same turns again as a second self.

The first self is still there. It does exactly what you told it to, every time, for as long as the heist lasts — so it can throw the switch on a door you have already walked through, or disarm a detector a moment before you walk into it, or carry a screwdriver back through the portal so that you are holding it turns before you reach down to pick it up. Three portals gives you four selves and four times the turns, all of them moving at once.

The second self can only ever do what the first self left room for, and you won't know what that was until you have played it.

The story

In 1928, you pick up a radio signal from five centuries away: a resistance movement losing a war against the banks. The same banks you can see from your window, small now, and patient. They want to fight them from the beginning, and for that they need someone in 1928.

Who should you trust? And why will nobody tell you what happens in 1929?

What you get

More than 50 hand-built levels across nine chapters, from a single-room tutorial to vaults that will take you an evening. It plays offline, there are no ads and nothing to buy.
